They dug themselves a palace beneath the sand,
with fake light and a pantry full.
Their laughter sounds cold, a stranger to pain,
while up above the people fight for warmth.
They've got eyes on every corner and metal bolts,
they play at being kings in their never-ending show.
But gold can't buy souls or a single ray of sun:
down there, their days are neon and booze.

They're the kings of filth,
they forgot about humanity;
with their lockdown and their greed
they built a bunker for evil.
Wake up, desert, no more keeping quiet:
the future is an echo telling us: enough!

They buy people at the mine like you'd buy a tool,
they shave their heads and keep a tally on them.

and they call it order, and they call it security.
They tell us they worked hard, that it's pure merit,
but their merit is a bunker bought on credit.
They're waiting for the imperials to split up the rest,
and our lives, to them, are nothing but a pretext.

they count their bread, we break it and share without fear.

we are the voice of a real change.
