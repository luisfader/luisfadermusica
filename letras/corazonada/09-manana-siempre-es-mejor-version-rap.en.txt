For you, my love.

Yesterday left its mark on my skin,
the ash of a memory that won't come back again.
The night was cruel and the cold was long,
but even the shadow has a sun in its charge.
The weight on my soul was a ton,
the road was decorated with fear and with nothing.
I close my eyes, I breathe in the calm,
and I hear a violin that mends my soul.

Tomorrow is always better:
with the dawn comes a new sun,
the wounds heal with its warmth
and love is born again.

The desert is huge and the thirst followed us,
in the sand we wrote what the fear had erased.
Every step, a heartbeat; every heartbeat, a burning;
every turquoise wall won out over fear.
And even if the echo of thunder rumbles on the hill
and doubt peeks out with spite from the corner,
there's a voice in the square that whispers in my ear:
there's no defeat as long as love hasn't gone.

Tomorrow is always better:
with the dawn comes a new sun,
the wounds heal with its warmth
and love is born again.

There's no endless evil and no pain without end,
every night has an edge, every winter a threshold.
I keep that one-of-a-kind strength in my chest:
after the storm, the sky is going to shine.
And if one day I get lost in the dust and the nothing,
your name is enough for me, like a hunch.
This verse is yours, every rhyme, every voice:
for you, my love.

Tomorrow is always better:
with the dawn comes a new sun,
the wounds heal with its warmth
and true love is born again.

Yes, tomorrow is always better…
For you, my love.
