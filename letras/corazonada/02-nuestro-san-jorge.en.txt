In the land of dust and eternal passion,
his horse is made of iron and its engine roars.
Saint George rises under the grey sky,
sword held high and the walls at his back.

The serpent advances, made of tank treads and steel,
it promises freedom, but it wants what's underground.
But he won't give in, he won't be swayed:
with his shield held high, ready to clash.

Oh, Saint George! Defend the Utopia!
With your shining lance, stop the tyranny.
The imperial serpent won't defeat us,
today we fight together — let's wake up!

The merchants of fear come to plunder,
with poison on their lips and orders to raze it all.
But Saint George calls the clans of the desert,
and those who were savages now march to meet him.

The serpent coils around the hill,
with drones in the sky and plundering missiles.
But with the sun ahead and the people at his side,
Saint George shines on this beloved ground.

Oh, Saint George! Defend the Utopia!
With your shining lance, stop the tyranny.
The imperial serpent won't defeat us,
today we fight together — let's wake up!

Crossing dunes of doubt, climbing mountains of fear,
the violin is his shield and the silence is thunder.
The glory belongs to everyone — we have to hold on!
The Utopia is the lighthouse, the path to follow.

Oh, Saint George! Defend the Utopia!
With your shining lance, stop the tyranny.
The imperial serpent won't defeat us,
today we fight together — let's wake up!

So, in battle, the spirit doesn't die:
Saint George knows it — with heart, it can be done.
With faith and hope, together to the end,
the united Utopia will never surrender.
