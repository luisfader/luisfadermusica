We walk the roads of a forgotten history,
we're the echo of a people that never goes out.
In lands of ash, under the cold of fear,
the hearts keep beating, and beat with no master.

We rise up slowly, we break the silence,
united in the fight, we defy your contempt.
The desert remembers, the memory is clear:
every blow we took forged the faces we wear.

Central Power, hear the outcry!
With fire and steel you sow terror.
Your law is death, hunger and pain,
but the people united have lost their fear of you.

From the caves of the south to the shore of the lake,
the clans and the fortress shake hands.
They come for rare earths with their armoured machines,
but every dawn finds us standing.

Central Power, hear the outcry!
With fire and steel you sow terror.
Your law is death, hunger and pain,
but the people united have lost their fear of you.

Eyes on the future, hands in the soil,
we sow life, we harvest the dream.
Rebellion is a song on the wind:
you feel it in the air, you live it through time.

The resistance grows like a river in a storm,
in every desert campfire the truth is burning.
From the ashes rise the dreams, the freedom we longed for:
every step we take is hope that's been sown.

Central Power, let your horror fall!
With love and with faith we raise the outcry.
Freedom is ours, the future is bright:
in every corner of the desert, a radiant people.

A song we sing together, one voice on the wind:
we raise turquoise walls… and inside, the dream.
