They gave you fire and you burned down the house,
they gave you the wheel and you used it as armour.
They gave you words and you built up borders,
they gave you the atom and you pressed the button.
You signed treaties in gunpowder ink,
you sold off the land, the water and the plants;
you swore "never again" with your hand on your heart,
and the very next day, the same old road.
Two years of winter, ash on the maps,
counting the days in chalk on the tin sheets.
And when the sun came back, as soon as you opened your eyes,
you were already handing out the spoils again.

Why do you fall again and again into the same trap?
Why do you go back to the fire like a moth to the lamp?
Once again someone has to bring you back to life,
once again we have to pull you out of the ashes.
Humanity… humanity…

Some dug down deep and made themselves kings,
others came on tank treads to dictate their laws,
and you, as always, looking on from the side,
waiting for someone else to pull you out of the mud.
They sell you fear wrapped up in a flag,
they charge you for the air, the shade, the waiting;
they tell you the free one is the one with the whip,
and you applaud from the most tragic side.
But out in the desert someone painted a wall
the colour of the lake, the colour of the future,
and inside there are no masters and no one to sell you:
there's bread shared out and an open door.

Why do you fall again and again into the same trap?
Why do you go back to the fire like a moth to the lamp?
Once again someone has to bring you back to life,
once again we have to pull you out of the ashes.
Humanity… humanity…

Humanity, dear old friend,
I know all your tricks:
you swear it's the last time
and you go back to the same street corner.
But tonight there's a campfire…
and someone saved you a place.

Why do you fall again and again into the same trap?
This time we won't let go of you: there's a light in the lamp.
Once again someone has to bring you back to life…
and this time, humanity, we'll teach you how to walk.
Humanity… humanity…

Get up.
