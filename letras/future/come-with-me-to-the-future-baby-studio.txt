(Street noise. A street musician plays guitar.)
(A woman, on the phone)
Yes, this is Svetlana…

(Alex)
I walk the streets, I read the signs,
I watch the people cross the lines.
A musician plays, the sun is bright,
I feel at home, I feel all right!
I see a girl, she says her name,
my heart begins a crazy game.
I know a line from an old romantic film,
it always works… I think… I will!

Come with me to the future, baby!
I have a machine — it's real, not maybe!
A thousand years, just you and me,
come with me to the future, baby!

(She)
I don't know you. Who are you?
(Alex)
I'm Alex, and I like you!
(She)
No. Please leave me alone.
I want to go into my shop, on my own.

(Alex)
Wait! Wait! I want to explain!
(She)
Stop following me! Again and again!

(Alex, quieter)
Come with me to the future, baby…
I have a machine… it's real… not maybe…
A thousand years… just you and me…
Why don't you smile like in the movie?

(Alex)
I just want to talk.
(The bookseller)
And she doesn't.
You need to listen.

(Alex, alone)
I look at her face, and now I see:
she isn't smiling. She's afraid of me.
I ask her for her whole life, in one minute, in one day,
and when she says "no", I don't go away.
I'm sorry, I'm sorry, I stop right here,
I walk away, the door is clear.
I don't explain, I don't say more:
a "no" is a "no". Now I know what it's for.

I don't say "Come to the future, baby",
I say "Hello", and I listen — maybe.
Love isn't a line from an old movie,
it starts when someone wants to be with me.

(Whispered, a clock ticking)
It's a quarter to one…
I have a card in my hand…
Somebody is waiting for me…
Maybe she has coffee.
